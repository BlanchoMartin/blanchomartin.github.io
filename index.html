About Me

Hello! I'm Martin Blancho, a passionate robotics and embedded systems engineer. I specialize in building intelligent, hardware-integrated solutions that bridge the gap between software and physical systems.

With expertise in C, C++, and Python, I enjoy creating innovative projects that combine electronics, programming, and mechanical design. My work focuses on developing efficient, reliable systems that solve real-world problems.

Projects

Autonomous RC Car

Converted a standard RC car into a fully autonomous vehicle using a Jetson Nano for onboard processing and a VESC for precise motor control. The system relies on a camera for visual perception and GNSS RTK for accurate real-time positioning.

Implemented real-time object detection using PyTorch models optimized with NVIDIA CUDA and TensorRT for fast inference. Developed custom path-planning and control logic tailored to embedded systems and real-world outdoor navigation.
